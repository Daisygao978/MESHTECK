Company Introduction

Mesh Teck (Jiaxing) Co., Ltd. is a manufacturing enterprise specializing in boundary products, including mesh fences, prefabricated garden fences, guardrails, automated protective nets, and access control systems. We integrate design, R&D, production, sales, and service. With the support of our sister company, Zhejiang Anrud Security Products Co., Ltd., we have over 16 years of experience in the boundary field, and our products are exported to countries such as Japan, South Korea, Indonesia, Mexico, the United States, and New Zealand.

Main Products

Our main products include:

  • Perimeter Protection System: Metal Mesh Series, Fence Series, Guardrail Series;
  • Automated Protection System: Wire Panel Series, PC Panel Series, Steel Panel Series;
  • Access Control System: Sliding Door Series and Swing Door Series;
  • Residential Security System: Courtyard Prefabricated Series;

Mesh Teck is committed to providing high-quality site safety protection products to the market and customers, offering professional and comprehensive site safety protection solutions.
