About Mesh Teck (Jiaxing) Co., Ltd.

Mesh Teck Co., Ltd. is a manufacturing enterprise specializing in boundary products, including mesh fences, prefabricated garden fences, guardrails, automated protective nets, and access control systems. We integrate design, R&D, production, sales, and service. With the support of our sister company, Zhejiang Anrud Security Products Co., Ltd., we have over 16 years of experience in the boundary field, and our products are exported to countries such as Japan, South Korea, Indonesia, Mexico, the United States, and New Zealand.
